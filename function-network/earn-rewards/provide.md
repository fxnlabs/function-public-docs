# Contribute Compute Power and Earn Rewards

Function Network empowers individuals and organizations to contribute their GPU and other computing resources to fuel decentralized AI inference. By sharing your hardware, you play a vital role in scaling cutting-edge AI infrastructure while earning on-chain rewards and gaining eligibility for exclusive network incentives.

## How It Works

Function Network leverages decentralized compute resources to power AI inference, enabling efficient and scalable hosting of AI models. By contributing your GPU or other computing hardware, you become an integral part of this ecosystem. Your resources help process complex AI workloads, ensuring that the network operates seamlessly and supports a wide range of decentralized applications. In return, you earn rewards directly on the blockchain, proportional to the compute power you provide. 

The process is straightforward: connect your hardware to the Function Network, allow it to handle AI inference tasks, and start earning rewards. The more compute power you contribute, the greater your impact on the network and the higher your potential earnings. This system ensures that participants are fairly compensated for their role in advancing decentralized AI.

## Rewards and Incentives

By participating in Function Network, you gain access to a range of rewards designed to recognize your contributions. Rewards are distributed based on the amount and quality of compute power you provide. These rewards serve as a direct incentive, offering tangible value for your participation in the network.

During testnet, contributors are eligible for exclusive points, which may provide access to future rewards or benefits as Function Network evolves. The more consistently you contribute, the greater your eligibility for these points, fostering long-term engagement with the ecosystem. For those who provide sustained or significant compute resources, Function Network offers tiered incentives, including higher reward rates and priority access to network allocations. This tiered structure ensures that dedicated contributors are recognized for their commitment to scaling decentralized AI.

## Why Contribute to Function Network?

Joining Function Network as a compute provider means becoming part of a transformative movement to decentralize AI infrastructure. Your participation helps build a more accessible, resilient, and efficient AI ecosystem, free from the limitations of centralized systems. By contributing your hardware, you directly support the development of next-generation AI applications while earning rewards that reflect your impact.

Beyond financial incentives, participating in Function Network positions you at the forefront of a rapidly evolving field. As decentralized AI continues to grow, early contributors like you will play a pivotal role in shaping its future, gaining both recognition and opportunities within the ecosystem.
